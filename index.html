Welcome to ChicKing India

Our Story

ChicKing began with a simple idea, to serve fresh, flavourful fried chicken that people can trust and enjoy every day. What started in UAE as a small restaurant has grown into one of the most loved halal quick-service chains, known for its crispy fried chicken, signature grills and diverse menu of burgers, wraps and many more delicious food options.

Across every outlet in India, our focus remains the same, providing consistent quality, warm hospitality and food that brings people together. Our journey continues with the promise of delivering better taste, better value and a welcoming experience for every guest.
